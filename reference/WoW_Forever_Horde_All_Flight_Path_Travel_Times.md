# WoW Forever — Complete Horde Flight Path Travel Times

**Snapshot:** November 14, 2026  
**Scope:** Horde-accessible flight masters only, including neutral flight points usable by Horde, camps, outposts, and new Forever flight points. Alliance-only stops are excluded.

## How to read this guide

- **Direct legs** are flight durations published by the community game-data route list; these are not live stopwatch measurements.
- **All destination times** below are the **shortest calculated sum of listed flight legs**. Entries with more than one leg are estimates, **not** independently measured end-to-end flights. Real routing, time overhead, and direction can differ.
- `via` tells you the intermediate connection(s). The calculation assumes you have unlocked every flight point in the route and the database's bidirectional connections work as listed.
- `—` means same location. Eastern Kingdoms and Kalimdor form separate flight networks; cross-continent boats and zeppelins are **not** included.
- Druids have an additional class-specific Nighthaven ↔ Thunder Bluff flight (8:30) that is **not** included in the general-population matrices.
- Times do **not** account for the Frequent Flier Legacy speed bonus. No player-level conditions or prerequisites are verified here.

**Data sources:** [WoW: Forever Wiki — Flight Routes](https://forever.undrwrldcub.com/db/flight-routes/) (direct connections, durations); [MaxDPS flight and transport map](https://maxdps.com/forever/travel) (cross-check for locations and faction accessibility); [Everything WoW flight paths](https://everythingwow.com/guides/forever/flight-paths) (supplemental Classic-derived comparison). Different sources occasionally disagree on exact durations; this file consistently uses the first source for its calculations.
